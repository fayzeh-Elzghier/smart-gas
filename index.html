01 / THE INVENTION

Rethinking the moment
gas needs to stop.

Smart GasGas safetyPatent application project

Smart Gas is an invention project centered on a smart safety system for closing a gas valve, with a permanent safety-lock mechanism.

We are developing the idea at Birzeit University and presenting our patent application project as an opportunity for conversation, technical feedback, and future collaboration.
